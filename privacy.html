FloatBase Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

FloatBase keeps everything you put in it on your phone. There is no account, no server of ours, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. FloatBase does not collect or receive your data.

What FloatBase stores

What you enter is kept on your phone, in two places:

  • The folder you choose when you set the app up. FloatBase reads and writes only that folder, through Android's own folder picker. If that folder is synced by another service (for example a cloud drive), that service's privacy policy covers the copy it keeps.
  • The app's private storage, a working copy of the same data, which only FloatBase can read.

What leaves your phone

Optional add-ons you install yourself may use the network, only when you use them:

  • Weather sends the name of the town you type to Open-Meteo to find it, then that town's coordinates to get the forecast. It never uses your phone's location. See Open-Meteo's terms.

Permissions

  • Microphone: only while you press the button to speak a reminder instead of typing it. Speech is turned into text by your phone's speech recognition service. On many phones that service is Google's and may process the audio on its servers, under Google's privacy policy. FloatBase never records or keeps audio.
  • Camera: FloatBase has no camera permission. A photo for a note is taken by your camera app and handed back.
  • Notifications, alarms, display over other apps, battery optimisation: so that timers and reminders ring on time, also when the phone is locked or in use. They read no data.
  • Internet: used only by the add-ons above.

FloatBase does not use your location, contacts, phone identifiers or advertising ID.

Backups

Android may include the app's private storage in your Google account backup or when moving to a new phone, as it does for most apps. You can turn this off in your phone's backup settings.

Sharing and deleting

Exports are written only where you choose. Uninstalling the app deletes its private storage. Your chosen folder stays yours: delete it to remove everything.

Children

FloatBase is made for working nurses and is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
